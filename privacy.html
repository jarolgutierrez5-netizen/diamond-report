Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains what information Diamond Report ("we," "us") collects when you visit diamondreport.app (the "Site"), and how it's used.

Information we collect

We don't require an account or collect personal information like your name or email to use the Site. When you visit, standard technical information is collected automatically, including:

  • Log data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited, collected by our hosting provider for security and performance purposes.
  • Cookies and similar technologies used by third-party services described below.

Advertising & Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show ads on the Site. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

Third-party data sources

Live game data, stats, and news headlines shown on the Site are fetched directly from third-party providers (including MLB's official stats API and ESPN's public sports API) at the time you load the page. These requests are made by your browser and are subject to those providers' own privacy practices.

Cookies

Aside from cookies set by Google AdSense as described above, the Site uses local browser storage (localStorage/sessionStorage) to remember your preferences, such as whether the sidebar is collapsed or which tab you last viewed. This data stays on your device and isn't sent to us.

Children's privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, with the "Last updated" date revised accordingly.
