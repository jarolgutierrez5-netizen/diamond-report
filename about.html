About Diamond Report

MLB analytics & projections, graded in the open

Diamond Report is a sports analytics site that turns live MLB season stats and matchup data into daily, model-driven projections — game winners, strikeout props, home run picks, and more. Every pick is generated the same way for every visitor, and every pick's result is tracked publicly, win or loss, so our accuracy is something you can check for yourself rather than take our word for.

Why we built this

Most sports projection content online is a hot take with no accountability attached — a pick gets made, nobody checks back on whether it hit, and the next day's take arrives as if the last one never happened. We built Diamond Report to work the opposite way: every pick comes from the same stats-driven process every time, gets locked before first pitch, and gets graded publicly against what actually happened. If the models are wrong, that shows up in the public record too — we don't get to quietly forget the misses.

What we do

Our models pull current-season stats, recent form, matchup history, park factors, and lineup data to project outcomes across ten different markets, from game winners to individual player props. Picks are locked in before first pitch using only information available at that time, and graded afterward against the actual result — never adjusted after the fact.

What we're building toward

Diamond Report started with baseball, and we're working on bringing the same approach to other sports as their seasons begin.
